Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

“The Missile Man of India”

A Life Dedicated to the Nation

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions of people through his dedication, knowledge, humility, and vision for India's future.

Biography

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He studied aerospace engineering and later worked with India's space and defence research organizations.

Kalam played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. His contributions to India's defence technology earned him the popular title “Missile Man of India”.

He served as the President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was widely admired for his simple lifestyle and his strong connection with students and young people.

Major Achievements

  • Contributed to India's space and missile programmes.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.
  • Inspired students and young people across India.
  • Authored several books including Wings of Fire.

Legacy & Inspiration

Dr. Kalam believed that young people could play a major role in building a better future. His life continues to inspire students, engineers, scientists, and dreamers around the world.
